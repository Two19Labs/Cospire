# UI audit and cleanup, 9 October 2026

Branch `feat/ui-cleanup`. Every screen of every role, measured rather than
judged: server latency per route, a real headless Chrome driven over the
DevTools Protocol, task flows timed click by click, and each screen looked at
against the approved prototype. Then the fixes, then the same measurements
again. No migration, no schema or policy change, no dependency, no change to
scoring, grants, access checks or the no-JavaScript fallback.

## Findings, ranked

### Critical

**C1. The page went blank after almost every save (fixed, `c8b2d88`).** Every
form ends its Server Action with `redirect()` back to its own screen with a
notice, which is what makes it work without JavaScript. With JavaScript, React
hands that redirect to the nearest error boundary, and the one Next.js puts
round every page renders *nothing* until the follow-up navigation has fetched
and drawn the target. Evidence: after "Grant access" on a mock there was no
`<main>` on the page 3.5 s later in 5 of 6 tries; after the mentor's "Save
component", 8 of 8; after renaming a question section, once; **on the deployed
URL** the page was still blank 10 s after a grant in 1 of 4 tries.
Fix: `src/shared/ui/action-boundary.tsx`, round every screen's content
(`RoleShell`) and the exam frame. It catches only redirect errors, follows them
exactly as Next does (push or replace in a transition, then reset), and keeps
the screen in view, dimmed and `inert`, until the target renders. Any other
error, not-found included, is rethrown unchanged. After: 0 of 6 and 0 of 4.

**C2. A save's redirect is sometimes never followed (mitigated, not fixed).**
The write lands and the action answers `303` with `x-action-redirect` in about
200 ms (measured inside the page by wrapping `fetch`), then Next's client never
dispatches the navigation: the button stays on "Removing…" for good. Locally
about 40% of "Remove access" clicks; on the deployed URL 2 of 4. Once in 41
plain client navigations the same kind of stall left a skeleton on screen for
over 10 s. Taking `revalidatePath` out of the action made it worse (5 of 8), so
that is not the cause. Root cause not found; it is inside Next 15.5's client
router. Mitigation: `SubmitButton` offers "Taking too long? Reload" after 8 s,
and a full load shows the saved state. **Needs**: a minimal reproduction, then
either a Next patch upgrade (a `package.json` change, so the owner's call) or
same-screen saves returning their notice through `useActionState` instead of
redirecting (touches every action; not attempted).

### High

**H1. The student home was a placeholder (fixed, `02eaae4`).** It told students
that courses, tests and feedback "appear here as their vertical slices are
delivered", linked three of six sections and said "Courses". It is now the
prototype's home: programmes with progress beside *Next up* (a test in
progress, the ARS round that is their turn, tests not started), then released
reports. Four reads in parallel, 237 ms warm against 278 ms for the old page.
The prototype's streak and average-score tiles stay out as prototype scope
(`design/README.md`).

**H2. The programme page waited on eight Supabase calls in a row (fixed,
`ca9e9a1`).** 505 ms warm (the item page 531 ms), the only routes over the
500 ms line. Sections, items, document titles, mock titles, progress and
attempts were read one after another. Now sections and items come in one
embedded read, titles in parallel, progress beside them: depth 8 → 3,
505 → 195 ms.

**H3. Headings did not say where you were (fixed, `2fe30e1`).** Ten admin lists
were headed with slogans ("Practice with purpose.", "Room to learn.", "People,
thoughtfully connected."). The mentor's skeleton said "Review queue" and the
page then said "Mentor workspace", so the heading changed on arrival: the only
wrong intermediate state the click recorder caught in 41 clicks.

**H4. The admin overview left out three sections (fixed, `2fe30e1`).** ARS
submissions, Analytics and the Activity log were reachable only from the rail.

**H5. The student's programme list showed no progress and did not look
clickable (fixed, `02eaae4`).** Now cards, each wholly a link, with the
prototype's progress bar.
