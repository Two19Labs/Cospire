# Agent Instructions - Cospire LMS

Before planning, editing, reviewing, or running commands, read these files fully:

1. `CONTEXT.md`
2. `CLAUDE.md`
3. The source documents those files route you to for the task

`CONTEXT.md` is the mandatory cross-agent handoff. At the beginning of work, add
or update your entry under **Active work**. Before handing off or ending a task,
record what changed, what was verified, what remains pending, and every blocker.

Do not store secrets, credentials, student data, or private client content in
`CONTEXT.md`. Do not overwrite another agent's active ownership without explicit
coordination. The signed agreement remains authoritative on scope, and the parent
operating manual remains authoritative on implementation.
